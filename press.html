Houston Chronicle

March brings restaurant openings to Houston — here's where to eat this month

March 2025

Rice Village is about to get a major upgrade. The old Sears building at 2400 University Blvd is being transformed into Memorial Trail — a food hall development bringing some of Houston's most celebrated restaurateurs under one roof. Among the confirmed tenants: Kata Robuchon, Uchi, Rosie Cannonball, BCNN, and more, creating a destination that positions Rice Village as Houston's next great dining corridor.

"The Memorial Trail food hall is set to redefine Houston's dining landscape — bringing world-class concepts to an already thriving neighborhood."

— Houston Chronicle, March 2025

Memorial Trail represents a significant investment in the Rice Village corridor, transforming a dormant retail anchor into a multi-concept culinary destination. The development aligns with broader trends in Houston's food scene — mixed-use food halls that bring chef-driven restaurants to walkable neighborhoods.

The project is being developed by a team with deep roots in Houston's restaurant community, with an emphasis on creating a gathering place that serves both the neighborhood and the city at large. Memorial Trail Ice House serves as the neighborhood bar anchor within the development — a craft beer and cocktail program designed to be the living room of Rice Village.
